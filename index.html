01 — PHISHING BASICS CYBERSECURITY EDUCATION

A Link That Tricks You

A phishing link is a fake or misleading link created to trick people into visiting a dangerous website. Scammers may pretend to be a bank, shopping website, or trusted company.

🎯
The Attacker's Goal: Often to silently steal your login passwords, private credit card numbers, OTPs, or personal money.
⚠️ Urgent Notification Just now

Dear customer, unusual activity noticed. Click here to confirm identity: http://secure-bank-login.xyz/verify

🪝
Phishing Trap
🤔 “Should I click this? It looks suspicious!”

02 — THE TRAP OF FEAR PSYCHOLOGICAL MANIPULATION

Click Now, Or Lose Access!

Scammers try to frighten you into acting quickly. They create artificial panic so you click before you have time to think, verify, or ask anyone.

🚫 “Your bank account will be blocked within 2 hours!”
⚠️ “Your account has been suspended due to violations!”
🔒 “Verify your credentials immediately to avoid penalties!”
💡
Golden Rule: Legitimate banks and services will never threaten to immediately shut down your account over a random SMS link.
🚨

ACCOUNT TERMINATION NOTICE

Time Remaining: 00:04:59
✋ PAUSE & THINK

Don't let fake panic force an accidental click. Real companies never use ticking countdowns.

03 — THE TRAP OF GREED TEMPTATION TACTICS

You Have Won a Prize!

Scammers offer fake prizes, free gifts, or unbelievable discounts. They hope excitement will make you drop your guard and ignore obvious warning signs.

📱 “Congratulations! You won a brand new Smartphone!”
🎁 “Claim your $500 free shopping voucher right now!”
💰 “You have received a special monetary reward from government!”
✨
Remember: If an offer sounds too good to be true, it is 100% a scam designed to harvest your personal information.
🎁
✨ 🌟 💸
⚠️ FAKE LOTTERY • CREDENTIAL THEFT

“Pay $5 shipping fee to claim your free phone” → They steal your credit card details!

04 — DECEPTION MECHANICS TECHNICAL TRICKS

Look Beyond the Message

Attackers use subtle visual tricks to disguise malicious web addresses so they look genuine at a glance.

1
Fake Clone Websites:

They create pixel-perfect copies of login pages (Google, Netflix, Banking).

2
Typosquatting & Lookalike Letters:

Replacing the letter l with number 1 (e.g. paypa1.example.com).

3
Suspicious Data Requests:

Unsolicited forms demanding passwords, PINs, or One-Time Passwords (OTPs).

✓ OFFICIAL GENUINE
https:// paypal.com /signin
Valid TLS • Verified Host
VERSUS
⚠ PHISHING LOOKALIKE
http:// paypa1.example.com /login.php
Letter 'l' replaced with number '1' to trick you!

05 — BEST PRACTICES CYBER HYGIENE

Pause. Check. Protect.

Developing simple, repeatable cyber safety habits will protect you from more than 95% of online phishing attacks.

✓
Do Not Click Unexpected Links:

If a message arrived unprompted, do not tap the link inside it.

✓
Inspect the Complete Domain:

Read the exact website address right before the first slash.

✓
Never Share Passwords or OTPs:

No legitimate organization will ask you for your private OTP via a link.

✓
When Unsure, Never Open:

Use a verified scanner like Check My Link to analyze the safety score first.

🛡️
✓

Zero-Trust Mindset

Always verify before you trust. Check suspicious links before they reach your browser.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

HOW PHISHING DETECTION WORKS

Four-tiered defense pipeline protecting users without executing untrusted code.

01
🔒

Safe URL Checking & Cleaning

Input links are checked for correct web format (HTTP/HTTPS). Private internal network addresses and dangerous script files are instantly rejected.

02
🧠

Smart Phishing Detection

Scans for fake brand spellings (like paypa1), hidden credential-theft keywords, and suspicious domain endings (.xyz, .top) used by scammers.

03
🛰️

Google Safe Browsing Check

Queries Google Safe Browsing threat databases in real time to see if the website has been reported for online fraud or malware.

04
📊

Clear Safety Report

Shows an easy-to-understand Red (High Risk) or Green (Safe) card with clear explanation bullet points. Dangerous links are permanently locked.
